In the year **1790**, the P4 source-window view returns four crown-role
candidates — two for Joseph II and two for Leopold II. This is **two
people**, and the year crossover is **not** a claim that they all held
these same offices on any one day. Leopold II's **Hungarian (1790) and
Bohemian (1791) coronations** are recorded separately from the rough
1790 accession-year window. That distinction requires historic sources,
not an automatic P39 interpretation.

The result is an **experimentally integrated date-qualified legal-office
timeline**, not a historical proof of uninterrupted control. The original
source photo and 150 historical provenance records remain preserved;
Wikidata source text never rewrites power assessments, creates a
collective "ruler" person or produces territorial polygons.

The published Pages source and allowlist do **not** include this SQLite.
The binary is distributed only as a 30-day Actions research artifact;
the text-based source and reproducible importer remain in Git.
