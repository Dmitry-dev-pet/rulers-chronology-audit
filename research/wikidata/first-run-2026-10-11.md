## Important discrepancy examples

- The world-pilot Leopold II of the Holy Roman Empire produced a first
  name-search result Q12967 (Leopold II of Belgium), while the appropriate
  historical figure is listed elsewhere among candidates as Q151321. This
  illustrates why first-result identity matching is prohibited.
- The world-pilot Frederick II of Prussia also returned Q130221 (Frederick II,
  Holy Roman Emperor) first. Identical regnal names are insufficient.
- Catherine II and Paul I each had multiple Wikidata birth-date statements
  whose comparison requires calendar/precision review (the report retains
  every statement rank and reference).
- Sophia Alekseyevna's birth date received calendar_review: a differently
  coded Julian/Gregorian statement is **not** an automatic historical
  contradiction, and we do not silently convert calendars.
- For all three linked people, the Wikidata death statement(s) had no
  corresponding canonical death assertion in the compared data layer.
  They remain a research queue, not newly verified deaths.
